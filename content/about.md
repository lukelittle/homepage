![Luke Little](/images/avatar.png#center)

I'm Luke Little. My career started in baking, moved into banking, and ended up in cloud consulting, and I wouldn't trade any of the detours. These days I build cloud and AI systems for banking and capital markets, and I spend a lot of my spare time helping engineers, teams, and students level up.

This site is where I write about what I've learned, what I'm learning, and what I think matters.

## What I write about

- **Regulated systems on AWS.** Architectures for the rules that keep markets safe: pre-trade risk controls, margin monitoring, best execution, and order lifecycle reporting. Start with the [Regulated Markets on AWS](/series/regulated-markets-on-aws/) series.
- **Applied AI and agents.** Hands-on builds with Amazon Bedrock, MCP, and agentic coding tools, with a focus on running them safely in environments where compliance matters. See everything tagged [AI](/tags/ai/).
- **Teaching the next generation of builders.** How students can get real experience before their first job: certifications, cloud projects, and a public portfolio. See posts tagged [education](/tags/education/) and [career](/tags/career/).
- **Community.** I host Season 4 of Ippon's [The Data Pour]({{< relref "/posts/2025/11/the-data-pour-s4e1-luke-little" >}}), give talks at universities, and I'm active in the Richmond AWS User Group. The [Talks](/talks/) page has all of it.

## Invite me

I enjoy speaking with students, meetups, and engineering teams about cloud careers, AWS architecture in regulated industries, and building with AI. If that sounds useful for your group, reach out.
